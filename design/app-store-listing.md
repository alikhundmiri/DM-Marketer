## Description
Found someone complaining about a problem you solve? Reply in seconds, in your own voice.

Tap Share on any post in X or Safari, choose DM Marketer, and get a draft reply about your product, based on the post and the thread around it. Copy it, paste it into your reply, send it yourself.

HOW IT WORKS
- Add what you're promoting once, in a few lines.
- Add a short note on how you write.
- Share a post. Pick a format: tweet reply, X DM, or email reply.
- Tweak with "write another version", then Copy.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- Drafts are written on your iPhone by Apple Intelligence. No account. No server.
- Your product notes and writing style stay on your phone.
- It never posts or sends anything for you.

ONE POST AT A TIME
DM Marketer is built for thoughtful, personal replies. It has no bulk sending and no automation.

TRACK WHO ANSWERED
Every reply you copy is saved. Tap "Got a reply" when they answer and see how many conversations you started.

PAY ONCE
10 free drafts to try it. Then one purchase unlocks unlimited drafts. No subscription.

REQUIREMENTS
Needs an iPhone with Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or newer) with Apple Intelligence turned on. Works best in English. Drafts are suggestions: check them before you send.

## App Review notes
- The app requires Apple Intelligence. On unsupported devices it shows an explanation screen and cannot be used. Please review on an iPhone 15 Pro or newer with Apple Intelligence turned on.
- Share extension: open any tweet link in Safari, tap Share, choose DM Marketer.
- In-app purchase: one non-consumable, unlocks unlimited drafts after 10 free ones.
- No account or login. No data leaves the device except fetching the public text of a shared post from X's oEmbed endpoint (and FxTwitter for threads).

## Privacy label
- Data collected: none by us. Say so carefully: the app sends a shared post's URL to X (publish.x.com) and FxTwitter to fetch its text. Check whether that needs disclosure; the safe answer is to describe it in the privacy policy.
- Needs a privacy policy URL before submission.

## Open questions
- Device filtering: the App Store cannot exclude iPhone 14 and older by Apple Intelligence support. Buyers on unsupported phones can purchase and hit the wall, so say the requirement in the first line of the description (done above) and consider the first screenshot showing it.
- "Writes like you" is a claim on a small on-device model. Keep the wording modest until you've seen real drafts.
- FxTwitter is third-party and unofficial. Decide if it ships.
